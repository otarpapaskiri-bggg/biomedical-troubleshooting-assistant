Diagnostic Engine

Generic engineering troubleshooting workflows

Equipment does not power on

Basic electrical troubleshooting sequence.

Step 1: Check the external power source.
Step 2: Inspect the power cable and connector.
Step 3: Verify the input voltage against the manufacturer's specifications.
Step 4: If safe and authorized, inspect the internal power supply and protection devices.
Important: Disconnect power before internal inspection. Follow the service manual.

Equipment shuts down unexpectedly

Basic power, temperature and protection checks.

Step 1: Record the exact shutdown conditions.
Step 2: Check the power supply and input stability.
Step 3: Review temperature and cooling system operation.
Step 4: Check alarms and error logs.
Step 5: Compare findings with the manufacturer's service documentation.

Abnormal temperature

Basic thermal system troubleshooting.

Step 1: Record the measured and target temperatures.
Step 2: Check temperature sensor connections.
Step 3: Inspect airflow, fans and cooling paths.
Step 4: Review alarms and temperature trends.
Step 5: Verify sensor accuracy using an approved procedure.
